# Product Context: FlowState Automations (v3)

## Vision & Purpose
FlowState Automations is an enterprise-grade AI conversational automation agency specializing in high-speed, deterministic & LLM-powered chatbots for Facebook Messenger, Instagram, and WhatsApp.

## Target Market & Ideal Client Profile (ICP)
- **Healthcare & Specialty Clinics (Flagship Demo Niche):** Dental clinics (braces, implants), aesthetic & dermatology centers, eye clinics, veterinary practices.
- **Professional & Local Services:** Law firms, accounting agencies, real estate brokerages, automotive detailing.
- **High-Ticket Retail & E-Commerce:** Custom furniture, bespoke apparel, specialized equipment providers.

## Core Technical Moat & Differentiators
- **Triple AI Provider Architecture:** Dynamic switching between Groq (Llama 3.3 70B for sub-second inference), Google Gemini Flash, and OpenAI GPT-4o-mini.
- **Natural Taglish Persona ('Mae'):** Built-in Filipino conversational empathy, polite honorifics (po/opo), pricing range guidance, and promotional consultation incentives.
- **Dual AI + Regex Lead Extraction:** Indestructible dual-layer extraction that reliably captures Philippine and international mobile numbers (09XX-XXX-XXXX, +639...).
- **High-Threshold Human Handoff:** Autonomous FAQ resolution with staff escalation on explicit dispute/emergency, auto-resuming after 15 mins of inactivity.
- **Meta Ads Speed-to-Lead Attribution:** Direct tracking of Meta Ad parameters (ad_id, ad_title) to connect closed appointment revenue to marketing spend.

## Pricing Packages
- **Starter AI Lead Bot:** ₱15,000 – ₱30,000 setup / ₱2,500 – ₱5,000/mo.
- **Growth AI + CRM Pipeline (Most Popular):** ₱40,000 – ₱80,000 setup / ₱5,000 – ₱10,000/mo.
- **Custom / Enterprise:** ₱100,000+ setup / ₱15,000+/mo.
