# Product Context: FlowState Automations

## Vision & Purpose
FlowState Automations is a business automation consultancy specializing in high-speed, deterministic Facebook Messenger chatbots and business workflow automation. 

## Target Audience
Small-to-medium businesses (service providers, local businesses, e-commerce, real estate, consultants) receiving repetitive Facebook Messenger inquiries who lose leads due to slow response times or staff burnout.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Instant Response (< 1 sec):** Engages customers immediately when buying intent is peak.
- **24/7 Lead Capture:** Never miss an off-hours inquiry.
- **Deterministic & Hallucination-Free:** Uses exact keyword matching and decision trees rather than unpredictable generative AI.
- **Direct CRM / Spreadsheet Sync:** Leads are delivered straight to the business owner's pipeline.

## Tone & Voice
- Authoritative, direct, modern, reliable, and outcome-oriented.
- Avoids fluff, hype, and generic marketing jargon.
