How It Works in 3 Simple Steps

No complicated software to learn. We set it up directly on your social media pages, and it runs automatically in the background.

1. Customer Messages Your Page

A prospective client messages your Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp asking about prices, opening hours, or location—even at 2:00 AM on a Sunday.

2. Assistant Replies in Seconds

The assistant greets them warmly in natural Taglish or English, gives exact pricing from your approved list, and politely asks for their contact number.

3. You Get a Booked Client

Their name, phone number, and appointment request are sent straight to your email or Google Sheet. You simply show up and close the sale.
